# A6.3 Author control and later methodological changes

> This reproduces Appendix 6 (A6.3) from the thesis.

AI assistance was used as a research and data-preparation tool. The author retained

control over the research design, the direct verification process, methodological
decisions, corrections, and the use of the resulting data in the thesis.

Review and normalization after provider verification

After the provider workbooks had been researched and verified, their fields were
consolidated into a common comparison workbook. The final feature layer contains 48

standardized variables for each of the 20 providers.

The normalization process separated:

   ●  the source evidence;
   ●  the current, partial, future, unavailable, or unknown feature state;

   ●  the strength of evidence supporting that state;

   ●  the verification boundary; and
   ●  the directional analytical value used in comparison.

A high-risk review queue covered 160 provider-feature records where negation, future
announcements, access restrictions, broad keyword matches, product exclusions, or

reverse polarity could produce a misleading result. A second contradiction review

Author responsibility for interpretation

The evidence packs and normalized workbook provide the empirical material for the
study. They do not determine the final thesis argument automatically.

The author remains responsible for:

   ●  interpreting cross-provider patterns;

   ●  deciding which comparisons are relevant to the research questions;
   ●  explaining the limitations of the evidence;

   ●  writing the answers to the research questions; and

   ●  drawing the conclusions.

AI assistance may be used for data organization, consistency checking, or language

editing, provided that its use is disclosed. The author checks the content and retains
responsibility for the submitted thesis.
