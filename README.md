Supplementary documentation for *Competitive Analysis of Non-Hyperscaler AI/GPU Cloud Providers: Capability Maturity Benchmarking and Strategic Positioning for Market Expansion* (2026).

- `methodology/dataset-generation-prompt.md` — condensed AI-assisted dataset-generation prompt reproduced from thesis Appendix 6, section A6.1. **This is not the complete original prompt.**

## Evidence and limitations

The documentation records the reported study workflow. Published provider claims, external reviews, and researcher observations are distinct forms of evidence. Console observation is not equivalent to successful provisioning or performance benchmarking. Source and feature states refer to the 2026 research cut-off dates, not live availability.

## Before publication

Review every file for confidentiality, account details, personal information, API credentials, and third-party reuse rights. Do not publish authenticated-console screenshots or recordings without checking their contents and permissions. Add the final workbooks and codebook only after verifying they match the thesis tables and calculations.
