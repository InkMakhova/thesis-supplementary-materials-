## Status and provenance

The PDF files above are **extracts from the thesis**, not the underlying workbooks. They preserve the published tables, but do not contain formulas, original provider-specific datasets, or the 2,061-row indexed source register. The original editable workbook(s), and any full verbatim generation prompt, must be added separately if the author elects to publish them. Do not state that those artifacts are available until uploaded.

Researcher observations were limited to accessible account/configuration views without paid GPU performance tests. A visible console or product page is not proof of successful provisioning, sustained availability, or production reliability. Current, partial, future, unavailable and unknown states must not be conflated.

## Privacy and publishing review

Before making this repository public, review material for API tokens, account details, personal data, proprietary business information, authenticated screenshots, and rights to redistribute third-party content. PDF extracts and AI prompts document methodology; they do not replace original citations or the author's verification.
